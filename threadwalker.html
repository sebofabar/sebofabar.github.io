THREADWALKER

Vogh wants Evollence gone. Evollence isn't done with him.

An original near-future science-fantasy thriller following Vogh and Yva through impossible incidents, dangerous institutions, and a collision neither sees coming.

In development Science fantasy Character-driven thriller
The premise

The aftermath is real.

Most of the world still looks ordinary. Then something impossible happens: a disappearance that makes no sense, a survival no one can explain, or evidence that contradicts everything the witnesses remember.

People disagree about what happened. Someone still has to deal with the consequences.

Evollence has the resources, expertise, and reach to do exactly that. Its work saves lives. But the corporation has interests that reach far beyond helping people.

Vogh knows Evollence from the inside—and has made his own decision about its future.

Yva has her own life, work, and reasons for being there.

They haven't met.

That is about to change.
