The premise

A world under pressure

Threadwalker is an original near-future science-fantasy thriller created by Sebastian Barnes (sebofabar). It follows Vogh and Yva through a world of escalating anomalies and institutional pressure, where survival does not guarantee freedom.

The near future still looks familiar from a distance: crowded streets, ordinary work, people making plans. Up close, the explanations begin to fray. Missing intervals, impossible survivals, and contradictory evidence leave real people dealing with consequences before anyone agrees on what happened.

Among those responding is Evollence, a powerful corporation active in medicine, technology, infrastructure, and emergency response. Its work can genuinely save lives. That doesn’t make every decision—or every claim of authority—easy to trust.

Vogh + Yva

Partners before symbols

Vogh and Yva have their own lives, their own loyalties, and very different ways of handling a world that keeps changing the rules. When their paths cross, the choices they make matter as much as the extraordinary things they can do.

Vogh

Vogh is an independent operator with a gift for finding routes where other people see dead ends. He has little patience for being managed, and enough skill to make that everyone else’s problem.

Yva

Yva notices what matters now and what will still matter tomorrow. Warmth and determination coexist in her; she has her own loyalties, ambitions, and reasons to fight.

The unanswered question

The LOOM

One name sits near the centre of Threadwalker’s unanswered questions: the LOOM. The story does not begin with a manual for the impossible. It begins with incidents people witness, the lives altered by them, and competing attempts to explain what happened.

Some explanations will prove useful. Others will fail. What the LOOM means—and how much anyone really understands—belongs to the story.
